AR Grid Overlay — proof of concept

Hold your phone up at the scene you want to sketch. This overlays a rule-of-thirds grid and a horizon guide on the live camera view to help you judge composition and proportion before you start drawing — no photo capture, no analysis, just a framing aid.

Checking WebXR support…

Requires Chrome on an ARCore-capable Android phone, served over HTTPS (or localhost on the same device). Not supported on iOS Safari.
